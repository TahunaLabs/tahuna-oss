# Code of Conduct

Tahuna's community should be welcoming, respectful, and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and assume good intent.
- Give specific, useful feedback about the work rather than the person.
- Respect differing experience levels, viewpoints, and backgrounds.
- Protect private information and avoid posting credentials or sensitive data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently ban
participation when needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns
privately to `hi@tahuna.app`. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports.

This code applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and other
official Tahuna community spaces.
